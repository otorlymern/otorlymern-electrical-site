Rehabs, Refurbs & Restorations

We rescue beloved gear that the modern market has written off as “too quirky to service.” From single-owner tape machines to road-worn synth modules, every restoration starts with a careful teardown, documentation of factory mods, and a plan that respects the instrument’s history.

Whether you need a museum-grade refurb, a stage-ready overhaul, or a gentle wrangling so the unit “just works,” we tailor the workflow to your budget and timeline.

What We Offer

  • Deep cleaning, recapping, and calibration of tape decks, consoles, synths, outboard, and video processors.
  • Faithful recreation or documentation of missing manuals, schematics, and patch notes to keep the instrument alive.
  • Modern quality-of-life upgrades (balanced I/O, power supply conversions, safety checks) while preserving original tone.
  • Beautiful restorations that retain patina—because your gear should look like it has a story.

How to Begin

Use the repair request form or email contact@otorlymern-services.com with photos, symptoms, and the vision for the piece. We’ll send a plan covering parts sourcing, lead time, and pricing tiers.
