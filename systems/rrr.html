Rehabs, Refurbs & Restorations

We rescue beloved gear that the modern market has written off as “too quirky to service.” From single-owner tape machines to road-worn synth modules, every restoration starts with a careful teardown, documentation of factory mods, and a plan that respects the instrument’s history.

Whether you need a museum-grade refurb, a stage-ready overhaul, or a gentle wrangling so the unit “just works,” we tailor the workflow to your budget and timeline.

Our Process

  • finding forgotten, junked, or otherwise unappreciated bits of vintage kit, and wipe em down and fix em up for studio use, whether they were intended for the studio or not
  • pull together everything we can find, documentation wise, on the thing not just for our use in the servicing process but to share with any of you that are wild about this stuff enough to be interested
  • make any necessary modifications to either bring the thing up to date, make it safe, or emphasize/access its potential as a piece of a/v gear
  • document the process and hopefully find the piece a good home when its up to snuff again!

Have something you need fixed up?
Have a old dusty heavy metal box with a lotta knobs and dont know what to do with it?

Use the appropiate form repair request form or email contact@otorlymern-services.com with photos, symptoms, and the vision for the piece. We’ll send a plan covering parts sourcing, lead time, and pricing tiers.
