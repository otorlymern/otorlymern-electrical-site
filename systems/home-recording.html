Home Recording

Home studios should feel inspiring, not overwhelming. OES builds analog-forward recording setups that welcomes collaborators, integrates DAWs without friction, and leaves room for experimental routing when inspiration strikes.

We combine vintage hardware, modern interfaces, custom patching, and affordable monitoring into systems that suit apartments, garages, and spare rooms alike.

Services & Support

  • Room-friendly console and tape machine integration with simple patch workflows.
  • Hybrid mixing chains that let you print analog flavor straight back into the DAW.
  • Modular synth, drum machine, and outboard racks designed for fast recall and collaboration.
  • Maintenance plans so your gear stays aligned, biased, and ready whenever you are.
