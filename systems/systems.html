OES A/V Systems

We offer products designed and handmade by us at the most affordable price possible, as well as refurbished and recontextualized vintage audio/video gear complete with handcrafted instructions. Vintage equipment can be intimidating—outdated nomenclature, unfamiliar I/O jacks, and functions originally built for a bygone era—so we simplify the use of vintage gear by modifying units for ease of use and compatibility with the modern home recording studio and creating easy to understand user guides to go along with the units..

Each vintage unit is thoroughly cleaned inside and out, parts are replaced only when necessary and modifications are made to simplify operation in the modern home recording environment—all while preserving the charming idiosyncrasies and aesthetic appeal of these vintage machines. Whether you’re an analog gearhead or a DAW based producer looking to add some analog warmth to your master bus, we aim to eliminate the intimidation of analog hardware by making interfacing with the units as simple and fun as possible.
