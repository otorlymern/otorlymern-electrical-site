Services

Otorlymern Electrical Services keeps analog dreams alive: we repair, restore, and reimagine audio/video gear while helping artists build studios, capture performances, and finish releases. Everything is sliding-scale and conversation-first—tell us what you need, and we’ll figure out a path that matches your budget and timeline.
