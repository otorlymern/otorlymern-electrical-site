Repair Request

OES provides reasonably priced, sliding-scale repair work for gear enthusiasts in the LA area. If you live elsewhere, you’re welcome to reach out as long as shipping won’t damage your gear. We specialize in tape machines (cassette or reel-to-reel), analog effects (rack units and pedals), vintage and analog synthesizers of all shapes and sizes, analog mixers—pretty much anything you’d find in a hardware-based home recording setup.

Email us at contact@otorlymern-services.com with your device, the issue, and your budget, and we’ll figure something out!
