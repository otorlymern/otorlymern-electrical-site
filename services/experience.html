Augustus Knight Calabro

Otorlymern Electrical Systems is Augie Calabro—artist, technician, and analog obsessive operating out of Los Angeles. The practice bridges tape machines, modular synthesis, projection, livestreaming, and site-specific installation, with an emphasis on keeping legacy hardware alive and bending it toward new contexts.
