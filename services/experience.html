Experience / Bio / CV

Otorlymern Electrical is the hands-on practice of engineer, archivist, and multimedia artist Augie—a lifelong builder of hybrid analog/digital studios and bespoke audio/video systems. From the first cassette four-tracks and circuit-bent toys to today’s modular synth caves and CRT video rigs, every project is grounded in curiosity, repeatability, and deep respect for the gear that brought us here.

Below is a snapshot of the recent work that informs our day-to-day service offerings. For a full CV, references, or project breakdowns, drop a note through any of the request forms and we’ll follow up directly.

Selected Experience

  • Owner and lead technician at OEStudio — analog/digital mix environment with modular video wing.
  • Archival restoration of tape machines, synthesizers, mixers, and video processors spanning the 1960s–2000s.
  • Custom hybrid systems: DAW-to-tape workflows, live playback rigs, gallery installations, and modular control surfaces.
  • Engineering and production for independent artists, labels, and interdisciplinary performers across the US and EU.

Recent Highlights

  • Consulted on immersive A/V installations for experimental art spaces in Los Angeles and Berlin.
  • Analog mastering and tape transfers for boutique labels specializing in archival and contemporary releases.
  • Commissioned analog video work featured at MicroLuxe, Human Resources LA, and community-run galleries.
  • Workshops and Gesprächskonzert-style demos covering tape maintenance, modular composition, and video feedback systems.
