Links & Resources

Curated references, manuals, and communities that keep the OES ecosystem alive. These are the links we send clients, students, and collaborators when they want to dive deeper.

Core Collections

  • OES Synthesis & Studio Library — scanned manuals, patch books, and technical guides for tape machines, synths, and more.
  • DIY Audio & Video Projects — schematics, build notes, and walkthroughs for classic circuits and OES originals.
  • DIY Pioneers Archive — documentation of influential designers, makers, and communities.

Trusted Knowledge Bases

  • Muff Wiggler / Mod Wiggler — long-form synth DIY and modular knowledge.
  • GroupDIY — pro audio builds, troubleshooting, and support for analog hardware.
  • Andreas Müller Lab — deep dives into video synthesis, signal flow, and feedback systems.
  • The Public Domain Review — archival media, graphics, and texts for audio/visual experimentation.
