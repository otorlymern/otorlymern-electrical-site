Audio & Synthesis Fundamentals

FileEditViewHelp

Start here: approachable texts on sound, instruments, and electronic composition.

  • Electronic Music: Systems, Techniques, and Controls — Allen Strange

    Essential reading for learning the synthesizer as an instrument. It reaches beyond most introductory synthesis books into self-playing patches, cybernetics, random sources, and much more.

  • ARP 2600 Owners Manual

    Probably the most essential synthesizer manual for introduction to synthesis, as the ARP 2600 was designed with synthesis education in mind.

  • Learning Music With Synthesizers

    An introduction to synthesis, written for musicians.

  • VCS 3 Synthesizer Manual

    A well-written manual that approaches the synthesizer as an all-in-one music studio while also teaching the basics of matrix mixers.

  • Serge Modular System Manual

    A comprehensive guide to using the Serge System, which once understood, basically gives you all you need to understand any synthesizer.

Synthesis

FileEditViewHelp

Patch books and deeper listening for shaping electronic sound.

  • Buchla Cookbook — Suzanne Ciani

    Performance-oriented patch ideas pulled from Ciani’s studio notebooks.

  • Practical Synthesis for Electronic Music — Roland Corporation

    Roland text teaching basic synthesis through patches, mostly for the SH-101 but can apply to any monosynth or synth system

Recording

FileEditViewHelp

Tape recording, maintenance, and home recording references.

  • Home Recording Tips and Techniques

    A series by Craig Anderton, mixed with a few other articles on home recording. It focuses on computer-free setups, but the techniques remain useful in a DAW-based studio.

  • Revox A77 User Guide

    Classic reel-to-reel manual for maintenance and alignment.

  • Yamaha MT4X Multitrack Cassette Recorder Owner’s Manual

    Operation and routing guidance for Yamaha’s four-track cassette recorder.
