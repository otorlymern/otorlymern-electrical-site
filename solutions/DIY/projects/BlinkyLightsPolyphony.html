Blinky Lights

Audio-reactive LED project

← Back to DIY Audio & Video Projects

Description

Three cascaded CMOS counters and decoders drive 24 LEDs in patterns at related rates. An op-amp clock, LFO control, or audio input can vary the display speed, making it a simple bridge between sound and visual art.

Open original source PDF ↗

Materials
  • Three CD4028 one-of-eight decoders and one CD4024 binary counter
  • LM3900/3900 quad op amp (as shown) and transistor control stage
  • 24 LEDs
  • Resistors and capacitors per the original schematic and parts list
  • 9 V supply; the article says 12 V operation may require bias adjustment
  • Board, wire, and a panel/display surface
Step-by-step guide
  1. Lay out the three LED banks.

    Plan three groups of eight LEDs. The source suggests parallel rows or concentric circles; each group advances at a different rate.

  2. Build the counter/decoder chain.

    Wire the 4024 clock counter and three 4028 decoders according to the schematic. Check each decoder output with one current-limited LED before wiring all 24.

  3. Add the clock and rate controls.

    Build the op-amp clock and R-18 speed control. Add the optional slow LFO path only after the base clock reliably advances the LEDs.

  4. Add the audio interface if wanted.

    Follow the source input amplifier/attenuator circuit. Start with a low audio level and set the top-speed limit to prevent the display from locking at maximum rate.

  5. Mount the circuit and LED panel.

    Use perfboard or the PCB approach described in the article. The source recommends separating the LED display and control board when useful for mounting.

  6. Set the supply and test.

    Begin at 9 V as specified. Test steady stepping first, then the LFO, then audio response. The article notes that 12 V operation may need R-11 adjustment.

The original article scan is the source for exact resistor/capacitor values. The legacy page contained likely unit transcription errors for capacitors; confirm values from the article before ordering.
