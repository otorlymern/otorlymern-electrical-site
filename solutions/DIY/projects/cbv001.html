CBV001 Circuit-Bent Video Enhancer

Eurorack video module construction

← Back to DIY Audio & Video Projects

Description

A 12 HP Eurorack adaptation of a circuit-bent video enhancer. CV controls the enhance level; four switchable glitch effects provide alternate image treatments. The source specifies 30 mA at +12 V, no -12 V or +5 V, and 25 mm depth.

Open original source PDF ↗

Materials
  • CBV001 PCB/kit and the current interactive BOM/source files
  • Resistors, capacitors/ferrites, semiconductors, trimmers, and connectors for the PCB revision
  • Eurorack 16-pin power cable and a suitable +12 V supply
  • Composite-video source/display or a compatible modular video path
  • Soldering iron, flush cutters, meter, and magnification
Step-by-step guide
  1. Confirm board revision and kit contents.

    The guide says kits with pre-populated SMD boards may skip passive and semiconductor placement and go directly to connectors. Match every part to the board revision.

  2. Populate resistors and passive parts.

    Use the build-guide placement drawings and BOM. Observe polarized parts; the guide distinguishes electrolytics from nonpolar ceramic capacitors/ferrites.

  3. Install semiconductors and trimmers.

    Match package orientation and pin-one markings to the silkscreen and guide images. Inspect for bridges and cold joints.

  4. Fit connectors and controls.

    Install input/output connectors, CV jack, switches, and potentiometers in the illustrated order. Check connector orientation before soldering.

  5. Inspect and power safely.

    Check for shorts and continuity before connecting Eurorack power. The module is specified for +12 V only at 30 mA; verify the ribbon orientation.

  6. Test video and controls.

    Pass a known-good composite signal, wait a few seconds for AC coupling to settle, then test enhance level, CV coupling modes, and each effect switch. The guide warns some settings can disturb sync.

Use the PDF’s visual placement maps and exact BOM; OCR does not preserve the tiny component labels reliably. Verify the current PCB revision and power connector orientation before applying power.
