Interactive A/V System

System design study · not a discrete DIY circuit

← Back to DIY Audio & Video Projects

Description

A historical article and block diagrams for interactive audio/video synthesis. It discusses coordinated sound and image generation, routing, modulation, and performance control; it does not provide a parts-level construction plan.

Open original source PDF ↗

Materials
  • Composite video source and display/capture path
  • Video processing modules with compatible signal levels and sync handling
  • Audio synthesizer or modular audio path
  • Patch matrix/mixer, control sources, and labeled cables
  • A notebook or diagram to map audio and video routing
Step-by-step guide
  1. Use the source diagram as a system map.

    Identify the audio generators, video processors, routing/matrix sections, modulation sources, and monitoring paths in the article.

  2. Build one working video path.

    Connect a known-good source through one processor to a compatible monitor/capture device. Preserve sync and confirm the signal standard before adding effects.

  3. Build one audio path.

    Connect an oscillator or other source through a mixer/effect path to speakers or headphones. Set levels low.

  4. Link control sources across both paths.

    Use one sequencer, envelope, or control source to vary a sound parameter and a visual parameter, following the article’s interactive-control idea.

  5. Add routing and document the patch.

    Expand one block at a time. Label inputs/outputs and record which changes affect sound, image, or synchronization.

  6. Treat the diagram as inspiration, not wiring instructions.

    The article is conceptual and historical; adapt it to the signal formats and safety limits of your actual equipment.

The supplied source is a four-page essay/diagram, not a complete build guide. It contains no discrete circuit BOM or modern device wiring instructions.
