DIY Syndrums

Three separate projects in one source packet

← Back to DIY Audio & Video Projects

Description

The archive packet contains three distinct percussion circuits: Syntom (touch-triggered pitch-sweep drum synth), Synwave (noise source, tunable filter, envelope, and VCA), and Synclock (a programmable rhythm/LED sequencer). Treat each schematic and parts list as its own build.

Open original source PDF ↗

Materials
  • Select one circuit and use only its associated parts list: Syntom, Synwave, or Synclock
  • PCB/stripboard, four controls for the Synwave version, sockets/jacks, and enclosure as indicated by that circuit
  • The source specifies a 9 V battery with a derived midpoint supply for part of the Syntom; follow the selected circuit exactly
  • Small screwdriver, soldering iron, meter, and an audio amplifier for low-level testing
Step-by-step guide
  1. Choose one circuit from the packet: Syntom, Synwave, or Synclock. Use only that circuit’s schematic and parts list.

    Use the circuit title and matching schematic/parts list. Do not combine parts across the Syntom and Synwave designs.

  2. Inventory and mark the board.

    The source gives separate board and case drawings. Identify that circuit’s controls, microphone/trigger or switches/LEDs, battery, and output connections before soldering.

  3. Fit passive parts first.

    Install resistors and capacitors according to the chosen layout. Check electrolytic polarity and mark transistor/IC orientation.

  4. Install semiconductors last.

    The Syntom article specifically recommends fitting semiconductors after the other parts and emphasizes correct orientation.

  5. Wire controls, trigger, and output.

    Use the original case wiring diagrams. Keep signal wires short and route battery/power wiring away from high-impedance input points.

  6. Test the selected voice.

    For Syntom, test touch/external trigger, pitch, sweep, decay, and volume. For Synwave, test trigger, noise, filter pitch/Q, and envelope response. For Synclock, test the 555 clock, 4017 sequence, and programmed LED/switch pattern in isolation.

This source packet is not one unified “Syndrums” circuit; it includes three designs and separate parts lists. Verify the selected schematic before assembly, and keep the Synclock switch/LED wiring separate from the drum-voice circuits.
