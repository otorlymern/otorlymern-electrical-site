Links & Resources

Helpful texts, sites, and videos on all things related to audio, video, and synthesis.

Intro to Audio / Synthesis Fundementals

  • ARP 2600 Owners Manual

    Absolutely essential reading for anyone interested in learning the synthesizer as an instrument.

    • Learning Music With Synthesizers

    A good introduction to what ill call sound science, written in terms musicians understand. Starts from the top assuming youve never touched a played an instrument or thought about how sound is so soundy all the time, then uses the fundamentals of how sound work to sneakly teach you synthesis

  • An Individual Note by Daphne Oram

    Killer book by sound/synthesis pioneer Daphne Oram, not "textbooky" at all, in fact, maybe putting this first is a bad choice, but she takes a wholistic look at the synthesis of sound and the creation of music, factoring in the composer, drawing compaisons between elements of composition and elements of human psycology andf existence. Nothing else like it!

    RIY: read more philosophy than you do text books, get bored if an author stays on one subject too long, like to free associate, feel being a synthesist/composer is a way of life
  • Composing Electronic Music By Curtis Roads

    This one is a text book. But in the best way. Roads doesnt shed is voice or bias's or opinions the way someone else writing something this Boldly titled might, thinking the most didactic approach would interfer with the facts the least. But thats what makes textbooks so goddamn boring ! Instead he writes about composing electronic music and even defines electronic music through a lens very much filtered by his experience pushing electronic music, defining electronic music, and paving the way for the buckets of filth that came crashing down

Synthesis

    Buchla Cookbook – Suzanne Ciani

    Seminal Buchla patch recipes direct from Ciani’s studio notebooks.

  • Bazille Cookbook – u-he

    Modular techniques for the Bazille semi-modular soft synth, transferable to hardware voicing.

  • Welsh’s Synthesizer Cookbook – Fred Welsh

    Systematic subtractive design from raw waveform to finished voice.

  • Buchla 100 Series Owners Manual

    Factory overview of the original modular system and its control philosophy.

  • Buchla Music Easel Manual – Allen Strange

    Performance-forward patching strategies for the Easel, annotated by Strange.

  • Electronic Music – Allen Strange

    The classic synthesis text: signal flow, studio methods, and composition strategies.

  • Composing Electronic Music – Curtis Roads

    Contemporary approaches to time, spectra, and algorithmic form.

  • The Complete Guide to Synthesizers – Devarahi

    Plain-language walkthrough of synthesis concepts with musician-friendly examples.

  • FM Theory & Applications – Chowning & Bristow

    Everything you need to program Yamaha-style FM from first principles.

  • Digisound Modular User Manual

    Project notes and module documentation from the Digisound DIY system.

  • The Complete Synthesizer

    Survey text spanning architecture, patching, and real-world studio considerations.

  • RTS Foundation

    Groundwork concepts and terminology—ideal as a structured refresher.

  • RTS Practical 1

    Hands-on exercises that reinforce fundamentals with compact, testable patches.

  • RTS Practical 2

    Follow-on studies emphasizing modulation depth, routing variations, and timbre shaping.

  • Serge Modular Music System Manual

    Patch philosophies and module documentation direct from Serge Tcherepnin’s system.

  • EMS VCS3 Manual

    Pin-matrix workflows and recommendations for the VCS3/Synthi family.

  • Roland TR-606 Owners Manual

    Original drum machine guide with programming workflows and specs.

  • Roland TR-909 Owners Manual

    Factory reference for pattern writing, MIDI sync, and service tweaks.
