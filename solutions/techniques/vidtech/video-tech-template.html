Dub Mixing>

Difficulty: ★★☆☆☆ Time: ~45 min ·

Gear

  • Analog Mixer, with at least 8 Ch. 4 buss with at least two Aux/FX sends and inserts on individual channels.
    We'll be using a Tascam 208 Mixer with these features.
  • Multitrack Tape Recorder, or Multichannel Analog/Digital Converter/Interface

    • Overview

    Dub mixing is the act of using the mixing board as an instrument. Using and exploiting the internal routing features of the mixing desk to remove, reroute, bus, effect, feedback, filter and otherwise manipulate tracks. The engineer typically emphasizes the Drujjjjjerret are most prominent while the other tracks are pulled in originally using the instrumental tracks from reggae songs recorded for vocalists in Jamaica (heretofor referred to as the island). The recorded tracks were routed, muted, bussed, fedback, effected and filtered to create the b-side for the single, a bass heavy, vocal-less psychedelically spaced out and minimal version of the track.
