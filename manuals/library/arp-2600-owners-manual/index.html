Why this document matters

The 2600 sits between a fixed-architecture synthesizer and a modular system. Its prewired paths make immediate sound possible, while patch points let those connections be interrupted, combined, or rerouted. The manual is valuable because it explains both layers together rather than reducing the instrument to presets.

For repair and restoration work, the operating guide also provides a behavioral baseline: what each section should do, how controls interact, and which apparent faults may instead be patching or normalization issues. That makes it useful before moving into schematics or calibration material.
