Why this document matters

The TR-606 is simple enough to appear self-explanatory, but its pattern, track, accent, fill, and synchronization modes are easier to understand as one system. The manual makes the machine's state changes explicit and is useful when a unit seems unresponsive because it is in the wrong write or playback mode.

It also documents the timing and DIN-sync context that connects the 606 to early Roland sequencers and bass instruments—useful for period-correct setups as well as modern clock conversion.
