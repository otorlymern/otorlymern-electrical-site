Why this document matters

The Buchla 100 was designed as an electronic music system rather than an imitation of an acoustic keyboard instrument. This manual shows that philosophy at component level: sources, modifiers, control functions, sequencing, and routing are presented as compositional materials.

It is especially useful when comparing East Coast and West Coast modular practice, understanding early control-voltage conventions, or tracing later ideas in touch control, complex timbre, and non-keyboard performance.
