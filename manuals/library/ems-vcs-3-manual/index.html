Why this document matters

The VCS 3 compresses a modular studio into a portable cabinet, with the pin matrix replacing patch cables for most connections. Its controls become much clearer when read as a matrix of sources and destinations rather than as a conventional left-to-right synthesizer panel.

The manual is also a useful historical reference for early British electronic music studios and for techniques built from unstable oscillators, feedback paths, joystick control, and non-keyboard sound organization.
