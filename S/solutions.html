OES provides solutions designed to empower everyone—from beginners to seasoned vets—to explore, learn, and create using analog audio/video hardware, DIY techniques, and accessible resources. We believe financial constraints, lack of prior knowledge, or limited experience shouldn't keep anyone from experimenting with sound, visuals, or circuit design. For newcomers, we offer clear, approachable guides and tutorials to start exploring immediately, even without specialized equipment. For those already immersed in these art forms, OES delivers detailed archival documentation, curated schematics, technical resources, and unique hardware, supporting deep dives into experimental audio, analog video synthesis, circuit bending, and beyond. Ultimately, our goal is to build an inclusive, knowledgeable community around the creative reuse of vintage technology—inviting everyone to rediscover forgotten tools and techniques, free from intimidation or gatekeeping.
